In short

Pensa works on your phone. You don't need an account, and there is no advertising and no tracking. We never collect your name or contacts. Only two things can ever leave the app, and only if you choose them: optional anonymous usage statistics, and a backup of your progress if you create an account.

What the app stores, and where

  • Training history (exercise results, levels, lesson dates, facts you learned, settings) is stored in the app's private storage on your device. If you create an account, a copy is also kept in your account (see “Optional account”).
  • Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No server is involved.
  • Language and preferences are stored on your device.

Optional anonymous usage statistics

When you first open the app (and anytime in Settings), you can choose whether to share anonymous usage statistics. If you say no, nothing is sent. If you say yes, the app sends simple counts such as "a lesson was started", "a lesson was finished", the type of session, its length in minutes and how many days the app was used, plus basic technical details: app and Android version, phone model, language, region, time zone, and the day and hour of use. These signals never include your answers, scores, the facts you learned, your name or contact details. They use a random identifier that is hashed on your device, so they cannot be linked to you. They are processed by TelemetryDeck GmbH (Germany, EU) for us, only to improve the app, and are never sold or shared for advertising. You can switch this off at any time, and sending stops immediately.

Backups and export

  • If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google backup so you can restore it on a new phone. This is handled by Android and your Google account, under Google's terms. We never have access to it. You can turn this off in your phone's settings.
  • You can export your data to a file you choose, and import it again. The file goes only where you save or send it.

Optional account

Pensa works fully without an account. If you choose to create one (in Settings → Account), we store your email address, a random account ID and a copy of your progress: the same data as the export file (exercise results, levels, lesson dates, facts learned and settings), plus the time of the last backup and the app version. The copy is updated after each finished session, so you can restore it on a new phone.

If you sign in with Google, Google confirms who you are and gives us your email address, plus the name and profile picture link of your Google account (stored with the login, not used by the app). We never get your password or contacts. If you sign in with email, we send a one-time code to that address.

This data is stored by Supabase, our service provider, on servers in Ireland (EU). It is encrypted in transit and only your account can read it. We use it only to keep and restore your backup: never for advertising, never sold, and never linked to the anonymous usage statistics. Signing out stops the backups; the copy stays in your account until you delete it.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Your data → Delete all in the app, or uninstall the app. Both remove everything stored by the app on your device.

To delete your account and its backup: Settings → Account → Delete account. Both are removed from our servers at once; the progress on your phone stays. You can also ask without the app: see Delete your account.

Purchases

If you buy a subscription, Google Play processes the payment. We receive only what Google Play provides to confirm the purchase on your device. We never see your payment details. When Pensa Plus is offered, Google Play's billing library may also send technical diagnostics to Google under Google's privacy policy. If you start a free trial, the app saves its end date on your phone, only to remind you 2 days before it ends.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. If you are under 16, ask a parent or guardian before creating an account.

Health

Pensa is a training and self-monitoring app. It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition, and it does not measure intelligence.
